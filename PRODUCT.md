# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

A four-person team (frontend, data engineering, ML, integration/product) competing in **Data Challenge: Doomsday** (Nazarbayev University, 7–8 November 2026). They work on laptops (all macOS/Linux) for two long days under time pressure, with a case from Freedom (financial services) that is revealed only on the day. Secondary audience: the competition jury, who see the team's results in Pitch Mode on a projector.

The job: take an unfamiliar dataset from upload to a defensible, presentable result as fast as possible, without the tool getting in the way.

## Product Purpose

ATLAS — Data Intelligence Studio (working name) is a local-first analytical workbench: upload → understand → explore → analyze → validate → explain → export. It exists so the team spends tomorrow on the case rather than on infrastructure. Success means the team can upload the revealed dataset within minutes of the reveal, trust what the profiling says, add a case-specific analysis module without restructuring anything, and present validated findings.

It must be fully useful even if the case needs no machine learning.

## Positioning

Every number carries its provenance. Charts, KPIs, model metrics, and findings always state the dataset, sample size, filters, and sampling they came from, so nothing on screen can be mistaken for an unverified claim. ATLAS never manufactures conclusions: automated text is limited to verifiable descriptive statistics, and interpretation is written by the team.

## Operating Context

- Runs locally: Next.js frontend (`apps/web`) + FastAPI backend (`apps/api`), launched with one command.
- Data stays on the machine. No external AI calls, no analytics SDKs, no uploads to third parties.
- Datasets arrive as CSV, Excel (.xlsx), or Parquet of unknown shape and size.
- Four developers extend the code in parallel on feature branches during the event.
- Final results are shown to a jury in a full-screen presentation view on a projector.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Dataset workspace: upload, preview, schema, profiling, data-quality checks, replace, delete, download.
- Explorer: filterable/sortable table, group-by aggregation, chart builder, saved views.
- Preparation: explicit, reproducible transformation recipes; original data is never modified.
- Model Lab (optional): classification and regression baselines with honest evaluation against a dummy baseline.
- Findings and Pitch Mode: evidence-backed findings, report export, keyboard-driven presentation.
- Challenge Module: an empty, documented extension area for the case revealed on the day.
- UI language: English.
- Unknown until the case is revealed (do not invent): the business problem, the dataset, whether ML is needed, the evaluation metric, the submission format, and the organizers' rules on prebuilt code and external services.

## Evidence on Hand

Only clearly labeled synthetic sample datasets (`data/samples/`, generated by `scripts/generate_samples.py`). There is no sponsor data, no real findings, and no real metrics; nothing in the product may present synthetic numbers as genuine insight.

## Product Principles

1. Correctness before polish: a wrong number presented beautifully is the worst outcome.
2. Disclose, don't assume: sampling, filters, detected-versus-assumed conditions, and model limitations are always visible.
3. Schema-agnostic: no screen depends on a specific dataset's columns.
4. Extend, don't restructure: tomorrow's case-specific work plugs into documented extension points.
5. Local and private by default.

## Brand Commitments

- Visual quality bar, set by the team on 2026-11-07: Linear-level craft (linear.app as a visual and UX reference only; no Linear code, logos, branding or assets). Dark-first: near-black and charcoal surfaces, subtle hairlines, one restrained accent, excellent typography and carefully designed charts. Every visual element must serve an analytical purpose; no generic SaaS templates, excess cards, gradients or decoration.
- The printable report stays on white paper; Pitch Mode stays dark for projection.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA as the working floor: keyboard-operable controls, visible focus, ≥4.5:1 text contrast, reduced-motion support, and charts readable at projector resolution.
